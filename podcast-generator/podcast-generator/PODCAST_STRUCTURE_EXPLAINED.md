# Podcast Structure

Each episode uses two speakers:

- Sarah: audit and risk management expert.
- Mike: governance specialist and researcher.

The generated script follows this structure:

1. Opening: warm introduction and why the document matters.
2. Understanding: main topic, methods or argument, evidence, findings, and conclusions.
3. Analysis: future risks, risk management, roles, assurance, governance, and practical implications.
4. Closing: key takeaways and final reflection.

The exact word count scales with the selected duration preset.
